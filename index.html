About ISPEC 2026

The 21st International Conference on Information Security Practice and Experience (ISPEC 2026) will be held on November 6-8, 2026 in Xi'an, organized by Xi'an University of Posts & Telecommunications and Shaanxi Normal University. The ISPEC conference is an established forum that brings together researchers and practitioners to provide a confluence of new information security technologies, including their applications and their integration with IT systems in various vertical sectors. The main goal of the conference is to promote research on new information security technologies, including their applications and their integration with IT systems in various vertical sectors. Previous ISPEC has taken place in Singapore (2005), Hangzhou, China (2006), Hong Kong (2007), Sydney, Australia (2008), Xi'an, China (2009), Seoul, Korea (2010), Guangzhou, China (2011), Hangzhou, China (2012), Lanzhou, China (2013), Fuzhou, China (2014), Beijing, China (2015), Zhangjiajie, China (2016), Melbourne, Australia (2017), Tokyo, Japan (2018), Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (2019), Nanjing, China (2021) , Taiwan (2022), Copenhagen, Denmark (2023), Wuhan, China (2024) and Chengdu, China (2025). For all sessions, the conference proceedings were published by Springer in the Lecture Notes in Computer Science series.

Author Instructions

Authors are invited to submit original papers: they must not substantially duplicate work that any of the authors have published elsewhere or have submitted in parallel to any other conferences that have proceedings or any journal. The submission must be anonymous, with no author names, affiliations, acknowledgements, or obvious references. Original contributions are invited up to 16 pages in length (single column) excluding appendices and bibliography and up to 20 pages in total, using at least 11-point fonts and with reasonable margins. Submitted papers must follow the Lecture Notes in Computer Science series (LNCS) template from the time they are submitted. Submissions not meeting the submission guidelines risk rejection without consideration of their merits. Accepted papers will be published by Springer in the Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) series.

To the best of our knowledge, to enjoy the 240-hour visa-free transit policy: 1)The applicant must hold a valid international travel document with a remaining validity of no less than 3 months and meet the entry requirements of the third country (or region) they are transiting to. 2)The applicant must hold a connecting ticket or relevant proof with a confirmed date and seat to a third country (or region) within 240 hours, fill out the Temporary Entry Card for Foreigners, and accept inspection and inquiry by the entry-exit border inspection authorities.

Foreign nationals who meet the above conditions may submit an application to the border inspection authorities at 60 ports in China, including Beijing and Shanghai. The border inspection authorities will process the temporary entry procedures for them. The visa-free stay period is calculated from 00:00 of the day following entry. Those who qualify under mutual visa exemption agreements signed with China or China's unilateral visa-free policy shall follow the provisions of those respective policies.
